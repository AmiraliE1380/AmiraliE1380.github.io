MSE student, Computer Science and Engineering, University of Michigan

Email Google Scholar GitHub LinkedIn

I work on compilers, program analysis, and automated verification. I am interested in how compilers can use automated reasoning, such as SMT solvers and model checkers, to optimize programs aggressively while keeping strong correctness guarantees.

My current work introduces super-analysis: an approach in which an SMT solver serves as an analysis oracle inside LLVM, proving runtime safety checks unreachable so they can be removed. One mechanism works across C/C++, Swift, Rust, and Julia, with speedups of up to 4×.

Before Michigan, I studied Computer Engineering at Sharif University of Technology (with a minor in Philosophy of Science) and worked on probabilistic verification at IST Austria.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
